FIX THE PRODUCT IMAGE GENERATION.

Do NOT generate any opening, open-product, unfolded, unzipped, cutaway, exploded, or interior-view images unless explicitly requested.

The product must remain in its normal, complete, closed state in every image.

GENERATE ONLY PROPER, USABLE PRODUCT IMAGES.

Each output must show the exact same product from a genuinely different and useful angle. Do not generate random variations, duplicate products, different colors, or altered designs.

- Proportions and dimensions
- Color and material
- Texture and stitching
- Handles, straps, hardware, and attachments

- All unique product details

IMAGE RULES:

ONE OUTPUT = ONE COMPLETE IMAGE = ONE PRODUCT = ONE UNIQUE VIEW.

No:

- Exploded views
- Cutaway views
- Duplicate products

Every image must show the product properly, completely, and clearly. The full product should be visible unless it is specifically a detail close-up.

DO NOT repeat similar front-facing images. Moving the camera only slightly does not count as a new angle. Every new image must have a clearly different perspective from the previous ones.

REALISM:

Create highly photorealistic premium e-commerce product photography. The product must look like a real manufactured physical product, not AI-generated, CGI, or a 3D render.

Use realistic materials, accurate geometry, natural texture, correct lighting, realistic shadows, physically correct reflections, sharp details, and proper perspective.

FINAL QUALITY CHECK:

Before generating each image, verify:
1. Is this the exact same product?
2. Is the product fully closed and in its normal state?
3. Is this angle genuinely different from previous images?
4. Is the product complete and properly visible?
5. Are there any duplicate products, duplicate parts, or unwanted variations?

If any answer fails, regenerate that image correctly.
