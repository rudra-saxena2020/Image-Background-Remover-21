Use this prompt. It is much stricter about **product scale, framing, consistency, and individual studio shots**, so it does not generate tiny products or multiple angles inside one image.

### Prompt

**Generate a complete premium e-commerce product image set from the provided reference product.**

The product must remain **100% identical to the reference** in shape, proportions, dimensions, material, texture, stitching, hardware, logo placement, colors, patterns, and all physical details. Do not redesign, simplify, distort, or invent any part of the product.

Create **separate, individual studio-quality images for each angle**, exactly like a professional luxury fashion or product e-commerce photoshoot.

#### Product presentation and scale

* The product must be the clear hero of every image.
* Product should occupy approximately **70% to 85% of the image frame**, depending on the angle.
* Do not make the product tiny, distant, or lost in excessive empty space.
* Keep comfortable margins around the product, but prioritize clear visibility and product detail.
* Frame the entire product naturally without cropping important parts.
* Maintain realistic proportions and consistent scale across all generated images.
* One product angle per image only. **Never create collages, grids, contact sheets, split screens, or multiple angles in a single image.**
* Do not add angle numbers such as "01", "02", "03", or labels.

#### Required separate product views

Generate each as an individual image:

1. Clean front view
2. Clean back view
3. Left side profile
4. Right side profile
5. Three-quarter front view
6. Three-quarter rear view
7. Top view
8. Bottom / sole / base view where applicable
9. Close-up detail view showing important material, hardware, stitching, texture, or craftsmanship
10. Interior / open view where applicable
11. Functional or capacity view where applicable, while keeping the product realistic and unchanged

#### Studio photography style

* Premium luxury e-commerce product photography
* Clean professional studio setup
* Soft neutral light gray, warm white, or off-white background
* Subtle realistic contact shadow beneath the product
* Soft diffused lighting with accurate highlights and material texture
* Sharp focus, high detail, realistic edges
* Centered and balanced composition
* Consistent camera height, focal length, lighting, background, and visual style across the entire image set
* Product should look naturally photographed in a high-end commercial studio, not artificially floating or rendered

#### Critical consistency rules

Every generated image must depict **the exact same product**, not a variation.

Do not change:

* Product shape or silhouette
* Size or proportions
* Color or color tone
* Material or texture
* Pattern placement
* Stitching
* Zippers
* Straps or handles
* Hardware
* Logo placement
* Number of compartments
* Product construction

**Important:** Each output must contain only **one angle or view of the product per image**, presented large and clearly, similar to premium product pages from luxury fashion brands. The result should feel like a consistent professional studio photoshoot, where the customer can swipe through each image to inspect the exact same product from every important angle.

**Do not generate a collage or several miniature product images inside one frame. Each angle must be a full standalone product photograph.**
