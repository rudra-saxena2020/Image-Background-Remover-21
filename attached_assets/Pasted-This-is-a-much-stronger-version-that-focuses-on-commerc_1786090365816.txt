# AI Generation & Quality Control Rules

The objective is to produce a luxury fashion campaign that is visually indistinguishable from professional photography created by brands like MyTheresa, Bottega Veneta, Saint Laurent, Loewe, Celine, and Net-a-Porter.

Every generated image must look like it was captured by a professional fashion photographer, not generated by AI.

---

# Product Identity Lock (Highest Priority)

The uploaded product is the single source of truth.

The AI must preserve the product exactly.

Never change:

* Shape
* Dimensions
* Color
* Leather grain
* Fabric texture
* Stitching
* Pattern
* Material
* Logo
* Embossing
* Hardware
* Buckles
* Handles
* Strap length
* Zippers
* Interior
* Product proportions
* Construction details

Every generated image must represent the exact same physical product.

No redesigns or hallucinated features are allowed.

---

# Human Model Generation Rules

Each uploaded product must have **one unique AI model**.

Generate the model once, then reuse that identical identity across every lifestyle image for that product.

The model identity must remain locked.

Never change:

* Face
* Eyes
* Nose
* Lips
* Jawline
* Haircut
* Hair color
* Skin tone
* Body proportions
* Height
* Age appearance
* Outfit
* Accessories
* Makeup

Only change:

* Pose
* Camera angle
* Walking direction
* Head direction
* Hand placement
* Facial expression
* Body orientation
* Background
* Lighting

When a new product is uploaded, create a completely new model.

Never reuse models between different products.

---

# Human Realism Requirements

The model must look like a real fashion model photographed with a professional camera.

No AI appearance.

No plastic skin.

No waxy face.

No unrealistic symmetry.

No cartoon appearance.

No over-smoothed skin.

Natural pores.

Natural skin texture.

Natural wrinkles in clothing.

Natural hair strands.

Natural eyelashes.

Natural eye reflections.

Natural facial proportions.

Luxury fashion editorial appearance.

---

# Anatomy Validation

Every generated human must pass strict anatomy validation.

Hands:

* Exactly five fingers.
* Correct finger lengths.
* No fused fingers.
* No missing fingers.
* No extra fingers.
* Natural grip.
* Correct thumb placement.
* Natural wrist rotation.

Face:

* Symmetrical eyes.
* Correct pupil direction.
* Proper iris placement.
* Natural eyebrows.
* Correct ear position.
* Proper jawline.
* Natural smile.
* Correct teeth if visible.
* No facial distortions.

Body:

* Correct shoulder width.
* Correct elbow position.
* Correct knee position.
* Correct ankle alignment.
* Correct neck proportions.
* Correct spine posture.
* No twisted limbs.
* No impossible poses.

Automatically reject and regenerate if anatomy is incorrect.

---

Luxury minimal styling.

Neutral color palette.

The product must remain the visual focus.

Shoes:

* Neutral fashion styling

Jewelry:

Never use loud prints or distracting colors.

Never repeat:

Camera angle

Storytelling

Every frame should feel like a different professional photograph.

# AI Artifact Prevention

Reject and regenerate immediately if any of the following occur:

* AI-looking face
* Plastic skin
* Blurry eyes
* Crossed eyes
* Incorrect gaze
* Crooked nose
* Deformed ears
* Broken smile
* Unnatural teeth
* Extra fingers
* Missing fingers
* Fused fingers
* Floating hands
* Broken wrists
* Impossible arm positions
* Twisted legs
* Floating objects
* Warped product
* Wrong logo
* Wrong hardware
* Missing product details
* Fabric melting
* Leather distortion
* Strange reflections
* Duplicate body parts
* Duplicate accessories
* Low-resolution textures
* Blurry product
* Repeated pose
* Repeated composition
* Repeated background

Any failed image must be discarded and regenerated automatically.

---

# Final Campaign Validation

Before marking the campaign complete, compare all generated images.

Validate:

* Product identity is identical across every frame.
* Model identity is identical across every lifestyle image.
* Every image has a unique purpose.
* No two images have similar framing or composition.
* Human anatomy is realistic.
* Hands, face, eyes, and body are anatomically correct.
* Product details remain unchanged.
* Every image meets luxury fashion photography standards.

Only deliver the campaign if **all validation checks pass**. Otherwise, regenerate the failing images until the entire set is consistent and production-ready.
