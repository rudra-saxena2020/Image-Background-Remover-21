## One critical correction

There is an even bigger issue in the current audit: **your validator currently uses COCO Faster R-CNN for product detection.** The audit itself says it checks person, handbag/backpack/suitcase, proximity, scale and a conservative identity proxy. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}

That is not enough for your actual goal.

It can answer:

> "Is there a handbag in this image?"

It cannot reliably answer:

> "Is this the exact handbag the customer uploaded?"

That's why the next implementation needs **two separate gates**:

```text
GATE 1
Is there a human carrying a handbag?
        ↓
COCO / object detection

GATE 2
Is it the SAME PRODUCT?
        ↓
Multi-reference product identity matching
````

And only:

```text
GATE 1 PASS
+
GATE 2 PASS
+
ANATOMY PASS
+
CONTACT PASS
+
SCALE PASS
        ↓
VERIFIED
```

Your audit explicitly recommends strengthening the identity verifier with multi-reference matching for the clasp, handle, eyelet, cord, silhouette and material. 

**That is the part I would prioritize.** Without it, Replit can keep producing beautiful AI photos that are technically "a handbag" but commercially useless because it isn't *your* handbag.
